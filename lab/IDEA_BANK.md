# Branching idea bank

Ideas here are candidate branches, not governing claims. They advance only
when they sharpen a causal contrast, calibration, falsifier, or useful task.

## Conditioning and choice

**Source intuition:** mealworm and other minimal-animal conditioning paradigms,
including first-order association, second-order transfer, reversal, blocking,
and contingency degradation.

**Possible contribution:** give the adaptive controller two bounded actuator
choices and a changing consequence relation, then test whether present
feedback produces transfer or reversal that a fixed schedule and yoked replay
cannot reproduce.

**Admission test:** specify observable cue, action, consequence, transfer
probe, fixed control, reciprocal yoke, and a result that would reject learning.
Phenomenal-consciousness claims begin with a separately defined test beyond
behavioral resemblance.

**Status:** the first-order reversal pilot is complete. The established policy
did not renew exploration after upper efficacy fell at concealed epoch 60,
which directly motivated consequence-change detection and a separately tested
renewal stream in the Rust controller. Second-order transfer remains a later
branch.

## Quantum comparison

physical mechanism.

**Status:** candidate note in `quantum-benchmark-candidates.md`; awaiting its

## Active field sensing and recruited material

**Biological comparators:** electric knifefish generate an electric field and
sense its deformation; hagfish release mucin and protein-thread skeins that
hydrate rapidly in seawater, recruiting the surrounding medium into a large
defensive structure.

**Possible contribution:** couple active electrical probing to a finite local
material reservoir so a detected field deformation changes where material is
released, widened, dissolved, or stabilized, and the changed body alters the
next probe field.

**Admission test:** name the emitted field, measured deformation, finite
reservoir, bounded material action, response latency, energy/mass budget,
fixed controller, and yoked replay. Test field-guided classical chemistry
first; reserve quantum-mechanism language for a perturbation that the
classical model cannot reproduce.

**Status:** retained branch available for a future admitted experiment.

## Offline cyber-gym choice benchmark

**Human-useful task:** operate a fully synthetic, seeded network gym with
partial observation, hidden topology, bounded defensive/offensive actions,
delayed consequences, repair, deception, and a fixed resource budget.

**Comparators:** fixed rules, seeded random policy, classical tabular or neural
Q-value learner, adaptive field controller, and a separately specified quantum
search simulator when the gym exposes a valid opaque-oracle subproblem.

**Measures:** environment steps, oracle queries, success, safe service,
containment, energy/compute, held-out topology generalization, and response to
deception or changed vulnerabilities.

**Admission test:** local purpose-built gym only; frozen scenarios and seeds;
no real hosts, credentials, scanning, payloads, or network targets. Classical
Q-values remain distinct from quantum state or quantum advantage.

**Status:** completed isolated candidate. The neural learner solved all 16
training and all 16 held-out synthetic scenarios; zero-shot reversal solved
none, while online renewal recovered 5 of 16. That boundary makes renewal,
rather than static fit, the useful connection to the substrate controller.
