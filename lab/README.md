# Private research lab

This lab separates parallel inquiry from canonical integration. Agents may
research, calculate, challenge, and draft inside their assigned workbench. Blu
alone integrates a result into the experiment or repository narrative.

## Objective

Test whether an AI-controlled conductive substrate can increase useful current
delivery while reducing local current, heat, and material stress, then maintain
or recover function under changing demand and damage.

The AI is the adaptive controller: it reads present fields, chooses bounded
optical or electrical perturbations, observes their consequences, and changes
its next action without prescribing the resulting anatomy. Fixed deterministic
remodeling is the control condition.

## Workbenches

1. **Materials calibration** — Earth-lab and vacuum/space material systems,
   dimensions, SI properties, oxidation/passivation, reservoirs, radiation,
   current limits, and manufacturable first experiments.
2. **Adaptive controller** — causal observations, bounded actions, objectives,
   invariants, fixed/yoked controls, controller state, and a minimal Rust/MOOSE
   interface.
3. **Falsification and statistics** — matched comparators, ensembles, power,
   damage schedules, leakage checks, reversal criteria, and the cheapest tests
   capable of disproving each claim.

Each workbench writes one concise document under `lab/`. It does not modify the
canonical README, simulation sources, results, or another workbench.

## Machine roles

- **Studio:** canonical repository, solvers, batch computation, and integration.
- **Pro:** Taylor's immediate visual and experimental control surface.
- **Intel:** ephemeral edge display; publish a short rolling view and forget it.

## Admission rule

A candidate enters the canonical experiment only when it names:

- material and environment;
- geometry and SI units;
- energy, mass, and control budget;
- fixed and adaptive comparators;
- measured outcome and uncertainty;
- a falsifier;
- and a reproducible path from input to result.

Normalized coordinates may support ensemble statistics internally. Public
figures and claims use physical quantities whenever calibration exists.

## Bounded serendipity

Exploratory runs may sample unexpected combinations of admitted materials,
stimuli, environments, loads, and damage schedules. Every draw records its
seed and complete candidate set. Invalid or unsafe combinations are rejected
before execution; unfavorable outcomes remain in the record; and no sampled
result enters the canonical claim until the falsification bench reproduces it
against a predeclared comparator. Randomness broadens the search without
becoming a route for cherry-picking.

## Fixed-rule control results

Keep the two controls separate:

- The canonical 24-epoch current-only remodeling run delivered 8.26 times the
  initial terminal flux with 1.43 times the material and 72.01% lower peak
  current density per delivered current.
- The experimental 36-epoch alternating two-fiber fixed-rule run delivered
  8.43 times the initial terminal flux with 1.44 times the material and 75.29%
  lower peak current density per delivered current. Raw solver fields remain in
  the ignored work area; its frozen metrics, exact cut–repair timeline, and
  pixel-identity visual evidence are promoted with provenance.

Both are fixed deterministic controls, not adaptive-AI results. Never pool or
silently substitute their metrics.

- Rill, Blu Private infrastructure, routers, and live sensory processes remain

- Literal lattice evidence remains available beneath every presentation; visual

  explicit tests beyond visual resemblance.
