# Simulation route

The simulation asks whether field-guided material growth can produce a
consequential present topology before any physical substrate is purchased.

## Five stages

1. **High-fidelity physics** — use validated phase-field and level-set models
   for electricity, ion transport, diffusion, heat, flow, and moving material
   boundaries.
2. **Small physical calibration** — use one transparent dish to measure the
   material parameters that must be determined experimentally.
3. **Model reconciliation** — require simulated and observed morphology,
   conductance, and time scales to agree within stated tolerances.
4. **Reduced Rust model** — preserve the calibrated relationships in a fast,
   inspectable simulator suitable for large parameter sweeps.
5. **Photon-choice comparison** — compare closed-loop AI/SI photon choices
   with an identical yoked replay before interpreting growth as interest,
   learning, repair, or autopoiesis.

## First powered comparison

The first predeclared paired bootstrap compares the unlit baseline with a
Cherenkov-cone field across 128 matched microstate seeds. Bridge step is the
primary endpoint; occupied cells and bridge success are secondary endpoints.
The scripts, paired results, decision rule, and power estimate are retained in
this directory and `simulation/results/`.

## First developmental answer

The guaranteed-transection study asks whether repeated complete path loss
changes later repair without supplying a replacement route. Across 64 seeds,
the paired first-to-final observed repair-time interval is entirely above zero.
Within this reduced model, accumulated topology materially accelerates later
reconnection. The voltage-routing MOOSE benchmark independently establishes
that the same developed topology remains poor at absolute DC current capacity.

At longer deterministic horizons, repair settles at a median 14 steps per
epoch and material approaches a bounded regime near 2,945 cells through 6,000
full transections. The compact horizon summary is retained in
[`results/recurrent-horizon-summary.md`](results/recurrent-horizon-summary.md).

## Documented failure boundary

[`whisker-ramp/`](whisker-ramp/) anchors the solid-path model to documented 10
V and 48 V failures, measured tin-whisker fusing currents, circuit impedance,
and the separate vacuum metal-vapor-arc regime.

## Installed local engine

The private Studio workspace uses the official prebuilt Apple Silicon MOOSE
binary. Third-party examples and generated simulation outputs remain under the
ignored `simulation/work/` and `simulation/output/` directories.

COMSOL's diffusion-controlled electrodeposition example is the preferred
high-fidelity reference model. Running it for this project requires a lawful
research or evaluation license; the UW teaching license excludes research use.
